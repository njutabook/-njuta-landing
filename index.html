about the book

every three months, get mailed a seasonal book that is beautiful enough to display on your coffee table and in your bookshelves.

simple with a purpose: to honor one moment at a time on the right, and to journal or scrapbook on the left as you please. part photo book, part journal. books are printed on italian fedrigoni uncoated text paper, optimal for both photos and writing.
