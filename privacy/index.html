2. On-device processing: your photo library

In Short: Your library never leaves your phone. Only the photos you approve for printing are uploaded, they are used only to make your book, and a human reviews the final print file before production. Uploaded source photos are deleted 30 days after your book ships; your finished book file is kept while your account exists so your shelf and reprints always work, and is deleted when you delete your account.

This section describes the complete life cycle of your photos, because it is the heart of both the product and this policy.

On your device. When you grant photo access, njuta scans and sorts your library — analyzing image quality, detecting faces, grouping similar shots, recognizing which people appear most often in your photos, and screening potentially sensitive images out of view — entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device frameworks. No photo, thumbnail, face data, or analysis result from this process is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Face-recognition groupings exist only on your device, are used only to improve which photos are chosen for you, and are never uploaded. If you never claim a book, nothing about your photos ever leaves your device.

When you claim a season. After you approve your book and pay, the app uploads the approved photos (approximately 30) to our servers over an encrypted connection, so that a printable copy of your book exists even if you never open the app again. If you later change your book — including during the adjustment window after your season ends — the app quietly uploads only the changed photos. Uploaded photos are stored in private, encrypted storage that is accessible only through short-lived, authenticated links scoped to your account.

What we use uploaded photos for. One thing: producing and shipping your printed book. We do not use your photos to train machine-learning models, we do not analyze them for advertising or profiling, we do not share them with any artificial-intelligence service, and we do not sell or license them. You retain all rights you have in your photos; you grant us only the limited permission needed to store, format, review, print, and ship them as your book.

Human review before printing. Before each season's books are sent to production, authorized Njuta personnel review the final print files. This review exists to (a) ensure print quality, and (b) screen for content that we or our print partner cannot lawfully or contractually print (for example, sexually explicit imagery or content that is illegal to reproduce). This is the only routine circumstance in which a person at Njuta views your photos. Personnel performing this review are bound by confidentiality obligations. We may also access your uploaded photos when you ask us to (for example, a support request about your order) or where required by law.

Locking and printing. At the close of your season's adjustment window, your book is locked and rendered into a print file. The print file and shipping details are provided to our print partner solely to print and ship your book.

Retention and deletion. The uploaded source photos are automatically deleted from our servers 30 days after your book ships (or promptly if you cancel before printing). Your finished book — the rendered file exactly as it was printed — is kept for as long as your account exists, for two purposes only: so your past books remain reliably viewable in the app, and so you can reorder a printed copy of a past season. Everything — source photos, book files, and your account — is deleted if you delete your account. Deletion is enforced by an automated process, not a manual promise.

3. Do we use artificial intelligence or machine learning?

In Short: Yes — machine learning is how njuta chooses your photos — but it runs entirely on your device using Apple's frameworks. We do not send your photos or personal information to any third-party AI provider.

njuta's curation — scoring image quality, detecting faces and near-duplicates, recognizing recurring people, and screening sensitive content — is powered by on-device machine-learning built on Apple's Vision and photo frameworks. This processing happens on your iPhone's own hardware.

To be explicit about what we do not do:

  • We do not transmit your photos, photo analysis, or personal information to any third-party AI or machine-learning service provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other model provider).
  • We do not use your photos or personal information to train, fine-tune, or improve any machine-learning model, ours or anyone else's.
  • We do not build or contribute to any facial-recognition database. Face groupings are computed and stored only on your device for the sole purpose of choosing better photos for your own book, and are deleted if you delete the app or revoke photo access.
  • We do not use AI to make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

8. How do we keep your information safe?

In Short: Data minimization first, then technical safeguards: encryption in transit and at rest, private storage with short-lived signed access, default-deny access controls, and strict logging hygiene.

Our first security measure is architectural: we avoid collecting what we don't need. Your photo library never reaches our servers; your card number never reaches our servers; there are no passwords to breach.

For the information we do process, we maintain technical and organizational safeguards including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest on our infrastructure;
  • Private storage for uploaded photos, accessible only through short-lived, authenticated, per-user links — never publicly listable or publicly accessible;
  • Default-deny access controls at the database level, so each account can access only its own records, verified on every request;
  • Authenticated, ownership-checked endpoints, request validation, upload limits, and rate limiting to prevent abuse;
  • Signature verification on payment notifications so payment status can never be forged;
  • Logging hygiene: our systems are designed to keep photos, full addresses, and credentials out of logs;
  • Secrets management: third-party credentials are held server-side only and are never embedded in the app;
  • Authentication tokens on your device are stored in the iOS Keychain.

However, despite our safeguards, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we cannot promise that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will never defeat our security. If a breach of security affecting your unencrypted personal information occurs, we will notify you as required by applicable law, in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay.

11. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your preference not to have your online browsing activities monitored and collected. Because we do not track visitors across third-party websites or serve targeted advertising, there is no cross-site tracking for a DNT signal to disable on our Services. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, and we therefore do not respond to DNT browser signals at this time. California law requires us to disclose how we respond to DNT signals; this paragraph is that disclosure. If a standard we must follow is adopted in the future, we will describe our compliance in a revised version of this policy.
