Cookie Policy

Last updated July 23, 2026

This explains how Njuta, LLC ("we," "us," "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit njutabook.com or use our services. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let the site, or a third-party service the site relies on, recognize your device and, in some cases, remember information about your visit.

Necessary cookies we use

These cookies are required for core features of our site to work — submitting a form, completing a payment. We currently rely on necessary cookies from two vendors.

Formspree

When you join our waitlist, your email is processed through Formspree, our form-handling provider. Formspree may set cookies necessary to process your submission securely and prevent spam.

Stripe, including Apple Pay

When you make a payment, Stripe — our payment processor — may set cookies to enable secure checkout, detect fraud, and remember your payment session. This applies whether you pay by card or through Apple Pay, since Apple Pay transactions are also processed through Stripe.

Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies required for waitlist sign-up or payment processing may prevent those features from working correctly. Formspree and Stripe each offer their own cookie controls through the links above.
